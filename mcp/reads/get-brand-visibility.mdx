Returns the brand's AI visibility summary from the AI assistants listed in `engines`, each with the date of its last answer. Includes brand visibility %, citation share, sentiment, top 5 competitors, top 5 cited sources, and per-persona citation rates. Optionally filter personaCitationRates to a single persona.

What the numbers mean:
- brandVisibility, citationShare and avgBrandRank are ALL TIME: brandVisibility is the share of all `answersAnalyzed` AI answers that mention the brand. windowDays does not apply to them yet.
- citationShare is the share of all URLs cited in those AI answers that point to the brand's own website or profiles, all time.
- topCompetitors[].mentionShare is each brand's share of all brand mentions in the last windowDays; the row with isYourBrand is this brand.
- topSources and personaCitationRates cover the last windowDays.
- sentiment covers the last windowDays and counts only answers that mention the brand. sentiment.score is the average of those answers' sentiment scores, each from -100 (negative) to +100 (positive); positiveCount, negativeCount and neutralCount split the same answers.
- trackedQuestions is how many questions are tracked; scanProgress is set only while a scan is running.

Use this when the user asks:
- "How visible am I across AI systems?"
- "Am I winning the GEO war?"
- "What competitors are beating me?"
- "How does persona X see my brand?"

Skip when the user wants the to-do list (use get_geo_priorities) or full brand identity (use get_brand_context).

Response shape: \{ windowDays, brandVisibility, citationShare, avgBrandRank, trackedQuestions, answersAnalyzed, engines[\{ code, name, lastResultAt \}], scanProgress, sentiment, topCompetitors[], topSources[], personaCitationRates[], lastAnalysisAt \}. Deprecated, kept for existing readers: totalPrompts and completedPrompts (now the tracked-question count, or scan progress while a scan runs) and topCompetitors[].visibility (same as mentionShare).

Example: calling with \{ windowDays: 7, persona: "SMB founder" \} narrows the persona section to matches of "SMB founder" (case-insensitive).

| Field | Type | Required | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `windowDays` | `integer` | no | — | How many days back to compute the summary. Defaults to 30. |
| `persona` | `string` | no | — | Filter personaCitationRates to this persona name (case-insensitive match). |
