title: "get_brand_visibility"
description: "Returns the brand's AI visibility summary from the AI assistants listed in engines, each with the date of its last answer."
---

Returns the brand's AI visibility summary from the AI assistants listed in `engines`, each with the date of its last answer. Includes brand visibility, citation share, sentiment, top 5 competitors, top 5 cited sources, and per-persona citation rates. Optionally filter `personaCitationRates` to a single persona.

## What the numbers mean

- `brandVisibility`, `citationShare` and `avgBrandRank` are **all time**: `brandVisibility` is the share of all `answersAnalyzed` AI answers that mention the brand. `windowDays` does not apply to them yet.
- `citationShare` is the share of all URLs cited in those AI answers that point to the brand's own website or profiles, all time.
- `topCompetitors[].mentionShare` is each brand's share of all brand mentions in the last `windowDays`; the row with `isYourBrand` is this brand.
- `topSources` and `personaCitationRates` cover the last `windowDays`.
- `sentiment` covers the last `windowDays` and counts only answers that mention the brand.
- `trackedQuestions` is how many questions are tracked; `scanProgress` is set only while a scan is running.

## When to use

- "How visible am I across AI engines?"
- "Am I winning the AI search war?"
- "Which competitors are beating me?"
- "How does persona X see my brand?"

Skip when the user wants the to-do list (use `get_geo_priorities`) or full brand identity (use `get_brand_context`).

## Inputs

| Field | Type | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `windowDays` | `int` | `30` | Window for competitors, sources, personas and sentiment. The all-time headline ignores it. |
| `persona` | `string` | none | Filter `personaCitationRates` to a single persona name (case-insensitive). |

## Response

```
{ windowDays, brandVisibility, citationShare, avgBrandRank, trackedQuestions,
  answersAnalyzed, engines[] { code, name, lastResultAt }, scanProgress, sentiment, topCompetitors[],
  topSources[], personaCitationRates[], lastAnalysisAt }
```

**Deprecated** (kept for existing readers, to be removed once no outside key calls this tool for 30 days): `totalPrompts` and `completedPrompts` (now the tracked-question count, or scan progress while a scan runs) and `topCompetitors[].visibility` (same as `mentionShare`).

## Example

Calling with `{ windowDays: 7, persona: "SMB founder" }` narrows the persona section to matches of "SMB founder".
