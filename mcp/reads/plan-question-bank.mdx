Call this first when the user wants to plan, rebuild, or expand the questions Citable tracks in AI search — or to see how many slots they have left.

Returns this brand's live bank: personas (who is covered and who has none), current questions, remaining plan slots, and, when connected, Google search demand (Search Console), not what people ask AI: the keywords people type into Google, with impressions and clicks. They do not show what anyone asks an AI assistant. A question you propose from a keyword is Citable's inference; mark it as inferred from Google search demand when you show it, never as a question people ask AI.

Then converse — do not dump this payload. Ask who buys, which competitors matter, and what prospects ask before buying. Propose a short list of new questions (who would ask each, and whether it is early research, comparing options, ready to buy, or already a customer). Wait for an explicit yes. Then call import_prompts with dryRun true.

If the new list should replace the starter set, propose archive_prompts first (also dryRun), then import.

Read-only. Call again after an import to refresh remaining slots.

_No inputs — call with an empty argument object._
