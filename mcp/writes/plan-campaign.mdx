Saves a project plan for one Opportunity map cell, named as get_opportunity_map returns it (rows, row, cols, col). Call get_cell_answers for the same cell first, and write the lanes only from what it returns. A cell has one project at a time: if it already has one, nothing new is made and you get its project_id to change it with revise_campaign_plan. It lands as a proposal: nothing starts until the team reviews every lane and approves it on the plan page. This is a WRITE: only call it when the user asked for a plan or a campaign ("Plan a project for …", "launch a Cyber Monday campaign") or confirmed your offer. The goal, sales, verdict and checkpoints are filled in from the brand's data; you write the lanes and the words. Channels: product_pages (Rewrite the pages AI reads when it answers these questions.); blog (Guides that answer these questions the way buyers ask them.); other_stores (Listings on the other stores that sell your products.); review_sites (Pitches to the review sites AI cites for these questions.); social (Posts on a social account you own.); community (Answers in communities like Reddit, as your team.); site_fixes (Fixes that let AI read your site: schema, speed, crawl rules.); chatgpt_ads (Paid placements in ChatGPT answers, bid on these questions.). A chatgpt_ads lane drafts the ads and stops: a person launches it, because launching spends money. Never write that the agent launches, publishes or sends anything.
