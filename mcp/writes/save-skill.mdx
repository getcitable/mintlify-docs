Saves a skill for this brand in Citable: a new skill, or a new version of one. Use it when the person asks to save, store or remember a workflow, or to change one of their skills. Every save is a draft: it runs nowhere until an owner or admin approves it in Citable, so give the person the link in the response straight away.

Writing a skill:
- name: lowercase words joined by hyphens, like "weekly-returns-check". Names starting with "citable" belong to Citable's own skills.
- description: what the skill does and when to use it, in the words the person would say. It's all list_skills shows, so make it specific.
- body: the steps, in order, as instructions another AI could follow. For the brand's facts and numbers, call Citable's tools (get_brand_context, search_prompt_intelligence, plan_question_bank) instead of pasting data, so the skill stays current. Write the case you just solved as a general one. Leave out customer data, passwords, keys and one-off details. Keep it under about 500 lines and put long reference material in files.
- files (optional): text files under scripts/, references/ or assets/. Sending files replaces all of the skill's files; leave it out to keep them.

Changing a skill: fetch it with get_skill first and send its latest version as base_version (0 creates a new skill). If someone saved in between, the save is refused and says which version is latest: fetch that one, redo your change on top of it, and save again. Send the whole new body, or edits (each \{ old, new \}, where old matches the current body exactly once) for small changes. Fields you leave out keep their current value.

Citable checks every save for things that look like keys, email addresses, phone numbers or tool names it doesn't have, and shows them to the person who approves it.
