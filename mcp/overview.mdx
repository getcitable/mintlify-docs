Citable's MCP server lets an AI assistant work with your brand's Citable data. It can show how AI assistants answer the questions your buyers are likely to ask, which brands those answers name instead of you, and which pages they cite. It can also draft fixes for you to review and approve in Citable.

**Endpoint:** `https://app.getcitable.com/mcp/v1`

## Connect with OAuth

1. Add the endpoint in your AI assistant's connector settings.
2. Sign in to Citable.
3. Choose the brands to connect and an access level. A tool can only choose among the brands you pick.

## Access levels

- **Read only:** see gaps, AI answers, your content and results. Read only can't create, publish or schedule anything.
- **Drafts for my approval:** everything in Read only, plus creating drafts and proposals in Citable. Nothing goes live: every draft waits for a person to approve it in Citable.
- **Read, write and spend:** everything above, plus actions such as publishing, scheduling and managing ads. This level works only on platforms we have reviewed. Connections from any other platform are limited to Drafts for my approval, whatever is selected at sign-in.

With Read only or Drafts for my approval, the connection cannot publish, schedule, spend your ad budget or contact anyone. Give Read, write and spend only to an assistant you trust to take those actions.

## Disconnect

Owners and admins see every app connected to a brand in Citable under **Citable MCP › Connected apps**, and members see their own. Any of them can be disconnected at any time. A disconnected app loses access within 30 seconds.

## Connect with an API key

For assistants that don't support OAuth, open **Citable MCP › API keys** in Citable (app.getcitable.com/api-keys-settings?tab=keys), choose **Create an API key**, and pick its access level (the same three levels). Keep keys private. To turn a key off, choose **Revoke**.

## Limits

- 60 requests per minute per connection.
- The data a connection returns reflects the AI assistants your Citable plan tracks.
